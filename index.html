Nandamuri Taraka Rama Rao Jr.

Jr NTR, also known as Tarak, is an Indian actor and playback singer who primarily works in Telugu cinema. He is known for his performances in films such as Yamadonga, Temper, Janatha Garage, Jai Lava Kusa, Aravinda Sametha Veera Raghava and RRR.

About Jr NTR

Nandamuri Taraka Rama Rao Jr. was born on May 20, 1983, in Hyderabad, India. He is the grandson of legendary Telugu actor and former Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. T. Rama Rao.


Jr NTR made his acting debut as a child artist and later established himself as one of the prominent actors in Telugu cinema. He is also known for his dancing ability, dialogue delivery and playback singing.

Career

Jr NTR made his lead-actor debut with Ninnu Choodalani in 2001. He subsequently appeared in several successful Telugu films.


His career includes a wide range of roles, from action and drama to historical and commercial cinema. His role as Komaram Bheem in RRR brought him international recognition.
